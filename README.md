# Hello, I’m Sasha Ohsumi

I studied Japanese at university and still enjoy exploring kanji. I’m also learning Chinese characters.

I’m building [ONKUNREN](https://github.com/veronello/onkun-kanji-by-hand), a project for reviewing the 2,136 jōyō kanji through handwriting practice. Writing characters by hand helps me remember them, and I like how it gives me a chance to slow down and focus.

I started ONKUNREN for my own review and wanted to share it with others who’d like to get back into the habit of writing kanji by hand. Some of the exercises draw on study materials I began putting together at university in 2013. I’m adding new exercises a little at a time.
