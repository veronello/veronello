# Hi, I’m Sasha Ohsumi

I studied Japanese at university and still enjoy exploring Japanese kanji and Chinese characters.

I’m building [ONKUNREN](https://github.com/veronello/onkun-kanji-by-hand), a project for reviewing the 2,136 jōyō kanji through handwriting practice. I started it for my own review and wanted to share it with other learners who also want to get back into writing kanji by hand.
